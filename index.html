Boolean Expressions

Greater than: A > B = A · B̅

Less than: A < B = A̅ · B

Equal to: A = B = A̅B̅ + AB = (A XOR B)̅

IC Information

IC 7408

Quad 2-input AND Gate

Performs AND operations to generate A greater than B and A less than B outputs.

IC 7404

Hex NOT Gate

Inverts A and B to provide complemented inputs A̅ and B̅.

IC 7486

Quad 2-input XOR Gate

Produces A XOR B. Its output is inverted to obtain the equality output.
